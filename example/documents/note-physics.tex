\section*{Kinematics}
\begin{law}
  A body stays at rest or in uniform motion if no net force acts on it.
\end{law}
The force is $\vect{F} = m \vect{a}$, and the work is
\[ W = \int_{t_0}^{t_1} \vect{F} \cdot \vect{v} \, dt. \]
